FAQ

Do my saves move to a new phone?

Saves live on the device. If you move phones with iPhone backup and restore or Quick Start, your saves come along.

Something I bought is missing?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple account.

Why do I wait for the next chapter?

The first two weeks play straight through. After that, each week-long chapter opens free after 24 hours, or sooner with an ad or an early-access ticket.

Ads won't load?

Check your connection, turn off any VPN, and try again.
