Privacy Policy

Last updated: launch day, 2026

The game needs no account and never asks for your name, phone number, email or photos.

Saves

Your progress, collection and settings stay on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

Ads

The free version offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Ads load only when you choose to watch one. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (from your IP address), which ads you have seen, and app performance and crash data, to show and measure ads. Before your first ad, your iPhone asks whether you allow tracking. If you say no, you can still play everything; ads just won't be picked by your interests. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Paid content is bought through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
